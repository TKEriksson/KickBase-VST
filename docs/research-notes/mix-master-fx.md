# Mix, master and FX

## Frequency and dynamics

**High-pass non-low-end parts** (about 100–120 Hz) — leaves kick/bass headroom; adjust rather than applying blindly to material with intended sub — *Our control/tool:* FX-channel EQ — 7 Rules; both PDF compendia [one source]

**Audit low-mid accumulation** (200–500 Hz) — synth layers and reverb tails collect mud here — *Our control/tool:* FX EQ spectrum and dynamic EQ; SAKNAS → dynamic EQ effect — Mixing & Effects Compendium [one source]

**Place brightness saturation above the low mids** (about 2 kHz+) — multiband drive can give leads presence without dirtying sub — *Our control/tool:* existing distortion/saturation FX; SAKNAS → multiband split drive — Mixing & Effects Compendium [one source]

**Use group glue gently** (1.5:1 cited) — a modest bus compressor can organise groups while preserving transient shape — *Our control/tool:* FX-channel compressor — Mixing & Effects Compendium [one source]

**Prefer timed ducking for rhythmic low end** (kick-envelope/volume shape) — a deterministic curve is more repeatable than a slow compressor detector — *Our control/tool:* SIDECHAIN kick-envelope/custom curve — Mixing & Effects Compendium [one source]

**Treat limiting as an audible transformation** (inspect delta and waveform; tune release) — a limiter can remove low-end weight or make cycle-level kinks, not merely add level — *Our control/tool:* MASTER clip/limiter/lookahead and scope — Unf*ck Master; What is causing [one source]

## Space, FX and pan

**Route long reverb/delay to sends** (20–50 ms pre-delay; return HPF 300 Hz, LPF 6 kHz) — keeps the dry transient defined and FX out of the floor — *Our control/tool:* FX1–FX11 send, EQ — Mixing & Effects Compendium [one source]

**Use dotted 1/8 ping-pong delay as a starting gesture** (tempo-synced) — supports a moving psychedelic answer around a short squelch/lead — *Our control/tool:* Echo/delay FX and send — 7 Rules; Squelches [one source]

**Automate grid motion** (1/16 filter opens, gates, pitch drops) — rhythmic background change can create density without adding permanent layers — *Our control/tool:* sequencer grid, LFOs, automation — both PDF compendia [one source]

**Keep kick/sub mono; spread only higher content** (below 100–120 Hz mono) — stable club translation; hats, arps and FX may move wide — *Our control/tool:* SOUNDSTAGE, OSC pan/spread, FX pan — Mixing & Effects Compendium [one source]

**Distinguish balance pan from stereo pan** (two-channel positioning) — a normal balance control can only attenuate one side of an already-stereo source — *Our control/tool:* per-OSC pan/spread; SAKNAS → independent L/R pan mode — How to Pan [one source]

**A/B every spacious effect in crowded sections** (especially across 16-bar transitions) — long delays/reverbs can join sections or erase definition — *Our control/tool:* FX bypass, sends, automation — 7 Rules [one source]

## Tool ideas

- Multiband saturator and dynamic EQ for FX channels.
- Stereo-pan mode distinct from balance, plus filtered reverb/delay send templates.

## Analyzer ideas

- Return-only spectrum showing 30–300 Hz leakage and 200–500 Hz build-up.
- Limiter delta waveform/low-band loudness comparison and mono/correlation monitor.
