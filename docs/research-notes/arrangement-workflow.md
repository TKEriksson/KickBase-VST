# Arrangement and workflow

These are production heuristics, not rules of the instrument. The supplied online-guide links remain unverified.

## Rules

**Make kick+bass the invariant** (repeatable hits; no accidental overlaps) — the low end is the reference frame for every later arrangement change — *Our control/tool:* deterministic seed, DUCK, Note end = Cut — 7 Rules [one source]

**Build movement in 16-bar blocks** (add, remove, automate, then bridge with FX) — repetition becomes hypnotic when a recognisable unit evolves rather than resets — *Our control/tool:* 1–64-bar pattern length, DAW automation, FX sends — 7 Rules [one source]

**Choose a primary emotional role per lead** (hypnotic, energetic, mysterious, or transy) — fewer simultaneous claims make a dense arrangement understandable — *Our control/tool:* instrument layers, filter/LFO and FX sends — 7 Rules [one source]

**Let the energy rise or fall deliberately** (section-to-section motion) — a static density plateau weakens transitions and climaxes — *Our control/tool:* LFO/automation, mute/solo, pattern variation — 7 Rules [one source]

**Use short, fast percussion at high tempo** (140+ BPM context) — clear transients and controlled tails leave rhythmic gaps — *Our control/tool:* sample length, envelopes, transient-oriented FX — 7 Rules; Mixing & Effects Compendium [one source]

**Allow an FX tail to cross a boundary only by choice** (e.g. delay/reverb between 16-bar blocks) — overlap can glue a transition, but must not spill into the low-end foundation — *Our control/tool:* FX send automation and return EQ — 7 Rules [one source]

**Use one tonal centre before intentional departures** (scale/mode basis; selective outside notes) — tension reads as a choice when a listener first receives a stable language — *Our control/tool:* piano roll; SAKNAS → scale highlighting — 7 Rules [one source]

**Time-box the first draft** (99-minute source workflow) — committing to an arrangement pass exposes structural problems sooner than endless sound tweaking — *Our control/tool:* SAKNAS → session timer / arrangement checklist — Producing a Track in 99 Minutes [one source]

**Use “interesting yet understandable” as a contrast test** (repeat a recognisable motif, alter one dimension at a time) — novelty without an anchor confuses; exact repetition without movement stalls — *Our control/tool:* duplicate pattern, A/B presets, variation lanes — Interesting Yet Understandable [one source]

**Treat guide links as leads, not evidence** (external links supplied by `guidesonline.txt.txt`) — open and verify before adopting their numbers or presets — *Our control/tool:* SAKNAS → source-link review list — guidesonline [uncertain]

## Tool ideas

- Pattern-variation lane for every 4/8/16 bars: gate, velocity, filter and FX-send deltas.
- Optional scale guide and section-energy annotations in the piano roll.

## Analyzer ideas

- Arrangement density timeline by frequency band and stereo width.
- 16-bar repeat detector showing what changed: notes, automation, FX return, or mix level.
